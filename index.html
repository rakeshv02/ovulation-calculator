This ovulation calculator predicts your most fertile days and likely ovulation date from the first day of your last period and your typical cycle length. It's built for anyone trying to conceive who wants to time intercourse well, as well as those tracking their cycle for awareness. Enter two simple inputs and you'll see your fertile window mapped out across the coming months.

How the Ovulation Calculator Works

Ovulation is the release of an egg from the ovary, and in a typical cycle it happens roughly 14 days before your next period starts — not simply on "day 14," which is a common misconception. The calculator estimates ovulation by counting back about 14 days from your projected next period, which it works out by adding your cycle length to the first day of your last period. From there it highlights the fertile window: the day of ovulation plus the five days before it.

That five-day lead matters because sperm can survive in the reproductive tract for up to about five days, while the released egg is only viable for roughly 12–24 hours. In other words, intercourse in the days leading up to ovulation is what gives the best chance of conception, not just the day itself. Because real cycles vary, treat the dates as informed estimates rather than guarantees.

Example 1: Last period started on the 1st with a 28-day cycle. The next period is estimated around the 29th, ovulation about 14 days earlier (around the 15th), and the fertile window spans roughly the 10th to the 15th.
Example 2: With a longer 32-day cycle starting the 1st, the next period falls around the 2nd of the following month, pushing predicted ovulation to about the 19th and the fertile window to roughly the 14th–19th.

Frequently Asked Questions

When am I most fertile during my cycle?

You are most fertile in the five days leading up to ovulation and on the day of ovulation itself, a stretch known as the fertile window. Ovulation usually occurs about 14 days before your next period begins. Timing intercourse across this window, rather than a single day, gives the best chance of conceiving.

How long does ovulation last?

The egg itself survives only about 12 to 24 hours after it is released. However, sperm can live in the reproductive tract for up to five days, which is why the fertile window is several days long. That combination is what makes the days before ovulation so important.

How accurate is an ovulation calculator?

This calculator gives an estimate based on average cycle patterns and assumes ovulation happens roughly 14 days before your next period. Real cycles vary from month to month, so the dates are a helpful guide rather than a precise diagnosis. For fertility concerns or medical decisions, consult a healthcare professional or use ovulation test kits.

Can I ovulate if my cycle is irregular?

Yes, but irregular cycles make the timing harder to predict with a calendar method alone. If your cycle length changes a lot each month, tracking basal body temperature or using ovulation predictor kits can pinpoint ovulation more reliably. Speak to a doctor if cycles are very irregular or you have been trying to conceive without success.
